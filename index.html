Methodology

How We Hunt

A battle-tested, intelligence-driven penetration testing process — transparent at every stage.

01

Scoping & Reconnaissance

Define scope, rules of engagement, and threat model. Passive OSINT, subdomain mapping, technology fingerprinting — before a single packet is sent.

02

Vulnerability Discovery

Manual and automated testing across all attack vectors — authentication, injection, authorization, business logic, and access control flaws.

03

Exploitation & Chaining

Real-world exploitation of confirmed vulnerabilities. We chain low-severity issues to demonstrate true critical business impact — like real attackers do.

04

Reporting & Remediation

Actionable report with CVSS scores, proof-of-concept, business impact analysis, and a prioritized remediation roadmap your devs can act on.

05

Re-testing & Verification

Free re-test after remediation. We verify every finding is fixed and issue a Certificate of Security Compliance upon successful closure.
